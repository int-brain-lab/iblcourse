# Preprocessing (slides)


## Extracellular recordings with Neuropixels
_Olivier Winter_ (~20 mins)

This presentation introduces the fundamentals of extracellular neurophysiology, specifically focusing on recordings using Neuropixel probes. It outlines key concepts like hardware, sampling theory, and the physiological basis of Local-Field Potentials (LFP) and Action Potentials (AP).

 [Link to the presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/19ErflK8HBzeZbK9hSlEuLzzIFdl0aCyl7wf9vio3Mh4)

## Sampling the extracellular voltage
_Olivier Winter_ (~20 mins)

This presentation focuses on sampling theory and hardware components such as analog-to-digital converters, Nyquist frequency, and grounding techniques. Additionally, it compares the technical specifications and structural configurations of Neuropixels 1 and 2 probes.

[Link to the presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1SdfpGozIqIvS4voiu7cJWfiNtOYYQnEk8TuKVRSvOvY)
